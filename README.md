## About Me

I design and build intelligent embedded systems that connect the physical world to the digital realm. Passionate about real-time firmware, hardware integration, and reliable IoT products.

- 🔭 **Focus:** Embedded firmware, STM32 / ESP32 / AVR, PCB design, and IoT systems
- 🌱 **Learning:** Advanced RTOS patterns, FPGA prototyping, and production-ready hardware
- 💼 **Role:** Electronics & Embedded Systems Engineer
- 📝 **Writing:** Technical articles on [DigiSpark](https://www.digispark.ir/author/alixahedi)
- 💬 **Ask me about:** C/C++, microcontrollers, sensors, communication protocols, and debugging hardware
- 📫 **Reach me:** [Alixahedi@gmail.com](mailto:Alixahedi@gmail.com)
